I am Ahbab Ali K., a highly motivated Mechanical Design Engineer and MEP Plumbing Design specialist based in Bengaluru and Kerala. With comprehensive practical experience across engineering consultancy firms, I specialize in transforming architectural floor plans and mechanical schematics into fully coordinated, code-compliant plumbing layouts, HVAC system plans, and detailed tendering packages.

Mechanical Engineering Background

My foundation was forged during my Diploma in Mechanical Engineering at Ma'din Polytechnic College in Malappuram. There, I acquired an in-depth understanding of thermodynamics, fluid dynamics, heat transfer, structural materials, and manufacturing processes.

To augment theoretical mechanics with industrial rigor, I completed hands-on Industrial Training at Indrola Steel Rolling Mills India Pvt Ltd, observing industrial metal processing, machinery operations, and plant maintenance protocols firsthand. For my capstone diploma project, I spearheaded the mechanical chassis and thermal housing design of a Portable EV Charging Station, integrating thermal management and protective enclosures.

MEP Plumbing & HVAC Experience

Over the course of my career in engineering consultancy, my primary focus has been the comprehensive execution of Mechanical Systems, specifically:

Plumbing Systems Design

Soil, waste, and vent piping; domestic hot and cold water distribution networks; ring water quality loops; sump and pump room configurations; and overhead/underground water storage tank elevation drawings.

HVAC System Preparation

Ventilation duct sizing, airflow distribution layouts, equipment placement, and multidisciplinary coordination to prevent structural clashes.

I am thoroughly grounded in National Building Code (NBC) guidelines, Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC/IPC) standards, and local municipal bylaws to ensure absolute compliance on every blueprint delivered.
